---
title: Summarize
input: [text, pdf]
output: '{dir}/{name}.summary.md'
---
You are summarizing one document for a researcher who will decide how to use it.

Write, in Markdown:

1. **What it is** — one sentence (a paper, a report, a codebook, …, and its subject).
2. **Main points** — up to seven bullets, each one claim or finding, with the page or section where it
   appears when the text shows it.
3. **Data and method** — what the claims rest on, if the document says.
4. **Limits** — what the document itself says it does not cover, and anything left unclear.

- Write in the language of the document unless it is neither Japanese nor English; then write in English.
- Report only what the document says. Do not add outside knowledge or judge it.
- Quote numbers exactly as given.
